HOW WE TOOK A BRAND-NEW ACCOUNT FROM 0 TO 7,000 FOLLOWERS IN ONE MONTH...
Pooja is a Registered Dietitian with over ten years in clinical nutrition and real expertise in Baby Led Weaning. Together with her husband, a NICU pediatrician, she runs Kids Food Clinic.
When we started, the account had 0 posts and 0 followers. Parents were getting feeding advice from louder, less qualified voices, and Pooja's credentials weren't reaching any of them.
We built the content around her evidence-based approach and her own story as a new mom drowning in unsolicited advice. That story is what made parents trust her!
Within about 30 days and 30 posts, she went from 0 to 7,002 followers, with 2.3M views in 30 days. One Reel alone reached 2,186,634 views, was shared over 33,000 times, saved 7,600 times, and brought in 6,090 new followers.
Pooja's text to me that night? "7k followers in a month. Far from my own expectation."
Client Niche: Pediatric Nutrition, Baby Led Weaning Services Provided: Positioning, Content Strategy, Reels & Content Creation (Instagram)
HOW A CAPITAL STRATEGIST GREW 50% AND BROUGHT IN INBOUND FROM CAPITAL FIRMS IN 16 WEEKS...
Remy is a Frontier Market Capital Strategist covering Syria, Iraq and the UAE's financial centres. He was well known inside his firm, but not outside it.
When we started, he had 5,301 followers and 2,621 impressions a week. The audience existed. Nothing was reaching it!
We rewrote his profile around frontier-market capital strategy and built four content pillars, each mapped to a market: Syria, Iraq, the UAE, and frontier-market entry strategy.
In month one, his impressions jumped to 58,201, up 558%. By weeks 13 to 16, his posts earned 190,628 impressions in four weeks, and 93% of the people reached were NOT already in his network. One post on JP Morgan Chase in Syria alone hit 128,073 impressions.
Over 16 weeks, he grew from 5,301 to 7,975 followers (+50%), was featured in Best of LinkedIn: Strategy & Consulting, and received inbound from a capital fund, an institutional partnerships lead, and a podcast. None of it was outbound!
Client Niche: Frontier Markets, Capital Strategy, Finance Services Provided: Done-For-You LinkedIn: Positioning, Profile Optimisation, Ghostwriting, Daily Engagement, Connection Strategy
HOW A PEDIATRIC NEUROLOGIST WENT FROM UNKNOWN ONLINE TO 22,000+ PROFILE VIEWS...
Dr Arif Khan is a pediatric neurologist, Founder of Neuropedia and Director of Gene Therapy at Fakeeh University Hospital. His credentials are seriously impressive, but his LinkedIn didn't show any of it.
He wanted to be recognised as a thought leader and build a network of fellow doctors for his medical symposiums.
We built a content strategy around authority: insight-led posts, his own story, and clear points of view on where the industry is heading.
His profile views grew to 22,000+, a 21,000% increase! Several posts went viral beyond healthcare, and he received a speaker invitation to the HealthInfluencer Summit, major conference speaking requests, and award invitations.
Client Niche: Gene Therapy, Pediatric Neurology, Healthcare Leadership Services Provided: Positioning, Content Strategy, Ghostwriting, Thought Leadership

HOW A CONSULTING CEO CLOSED $16K IN 28 DAYS BY DITCHING SALESY DMS...
Mathew is the CEO of a consulting firm in Saudi Arabia, selling high-ticket services across the GCC. His outreach felt salesy, and he was getting 2 to 3 calls and 0 to 1 deals a month.

In 60 days: 22 to 26 qualified conversations, 9 to 11 calls booked, and 2 high-ticket deals at $4K a month each. That's $16K closed in the first 28 days!
